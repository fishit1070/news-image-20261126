This entry squeezes a whole day of world news onto a single newspaper front page: Thursday 26 November 2026, summed up in one image. The date itself is the largest text on the page, and directly beneath the masthead runs a strip of the day's six defining numbers — 100 years of the Macy's parade, a €45bn EU loan proposed for Ukraine, 12,021 confirmed Ebola cases in DR Congo, 100,000t of grain aboard two hit ships, R$4.3bn blocked in a São Paulo fraud probe, and 20% of LNG output reserved for homes. Below that, 13 numbered story cards each carry a location kicker and a field tag, followed by a two-row UTC timeline of the day's events. A DATE CHECK box keeps the page honest by separating stories that were deliberately excluded, with their real dates, from items that simply had not happened yet, and an ALSO column rounds out the coverage across 30 numbered slots spanning 9 regions. Every claim is graded by the verification legend printed at the foot of the page: CONFIRMED, OFFICIAL, or REPORTED.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`).
